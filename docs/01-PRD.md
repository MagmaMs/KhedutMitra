# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Product Overview
**KhedutMitra** (Farmer's Friend) is an integrated digital platform designed to empower Indian farmers by providing real-time data, community support, AI advisory, and direct market access. Built as a comprehensive hackathon submission for CodeCraft '26, it aims to eliminate information asymmetry and middlemen.

## 2. Mission
To democratize access to agricultural intelligence and equitable markets for every Indian farmer through accessible, localized technology.

## 3. User Personas

### 3.1 The Farmer (Primary)
- **Profile:** Small to medium landholding, comfortable with basic smartphone usage, prefers regional languages.
- **Pain Points:** Unpredictable weather, lack of timely disease identification, volatile market prices, exploitation by middlemen.
- **Goals:** Increase yield, reduce input costs, secure better prices for crops.

### 3.2 The Consumer/Buyer (Secondary)
- **Profile:** Retail buyers, wholesale merchants, or restaurants seeking fresh produce.
- **Pain Points:** Inconsistent supply, high markups by intermediaries, lack of traceability.
- **Goals:** Direct access to fresh, fairly priced agricultural produce.

## 4. Key Features
1. **Multilingual Support:** English, Hindi, and Gujarati via robust i18n strategy.
2. **Weather & Advisory:** Hyperlocal Open-Meteo integration with AI-driven crop advisory.
3. **Market Module:** Real-time AGMARKNET price tracking and direct farmer-to-consumer crop listings.
4. **Disease Tracker:** ML-based plant disease detection via image upload.
5. **Community Forum:** Peer-to-peer knowledge sharing and expert Q&A.
6. **Schemes Directory:** Consolidated government agricultural schemes.

## 5. Success Metrics
- Daily Active Users (DAU) to Monthly Active Users (MAU) ratio.
- Number of successful direct market transactions.
- Disease detection accuracy and user adoption rate.
- Engagement in the Community module.
