# CodeCraft '26 Hackathon Pitch

## 1. The Hook (0:00 - 0:30)
*Did you know that despite feeding 1.4 billion people, the average Indian farmer earns less than ₹10,000 a month? Much of their profit is lost to unpredictable weather, undiagnosed crop diseases, and exploitative middlemen.*

## 2. The Solution: KhedutMitra (0:30 - 1:30)
Meet **KhedutMitra**—the holistic digital companion for the modern Indian farmer. It’s not just an app; it's an ecosystem.
- **Localized:** Speaks their language (Hindi, Gujarati, English).
- **Intelligent:** AI-driven crop advisory and instant ML disease detection.
- **Equitable:** Bypasses middlemen by connecting farmers directly to consumers for fresh, fair-priced produce.

## 3. The Demo (1:30 - 3:30)
*Walkthrough of the application:*
1. Show seamless Phone Auth.
2. Demonstrate the Gujarati translation toggle.
3. Show the hyper-local weather and AI advice generation.
4. Take a photo of a diseased leaf (use a sample image) and get instant treatment advice.
5. Create a crop listing as a Farmer, then switch to a Consumer view to purchase it.

## 4. The Tech Stack (3:30 - 4:15)
Built for speed and scale:
- React & Vite for a snappy UI.
- Supabase for secure, real-time relational data.
- Express gateway integrating Open-Meteo and LLMs.
- Magic Patterns design system for a polished, accessible UX.

## 5. Impact & Future (4:15 - 5:00)
By eliminating information asymmetry, KhedutMitra doesn't just improve yields; it improves lives. Post-hackathon, we plan to integrate regional voice assistants and logistics partners.
